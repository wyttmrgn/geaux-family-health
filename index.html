About the clinic

One clinic for the baby’s first checkup, the teenager’s sports physical and Grandpa’s blood pressure.

Family practice means we treat the whole household, not one age group. Our nurse practitioners see newborns, school-age kids, working parents and retirees, and a lot of the time they are all related.

Seeing the same providers year after year matters. They know your history and what has changed since your last visit, so you are not starting over with someone new each time.

Often seen for

  • Annual checkups
  • Sick visits
  • Diabetes
  • High blood pressure
  • Anxiety
  • Immunizations
  • Allergies
  • School & sports physicals

Care for every stage of life.

We look after patients from their first week to their ninth decade. Here is what visits usually cover at each age.

Infants & toddlers

Birth to 3

Well-baby checkups, growth tracking and the first rounds of shots, with plenty of time for a new parent’s questions.

  • Well-baby visits
  • Immunizations
  • Growth & milestones
  • Sick visits

Kids & teens

4 to 17

Physicals for school and sports, sick days, and the fevers and sore throats that never wait for a good time.

  • School & sports physicals
  • Colds, flu & strep
  • Immunizations
  • Asthma & allergies

Adults

18 to 64

A yearly wellness exam, and steady help with the conditions that need regular attention.

  • Annual wellness exams
  • Diabetes care
  • Blood pressure & cholesterol
  • Anxiety & depression

Seniors

65 and up

Regular visits to keep medications, long-term conditions and screenings on track, at an unhurried pace.

  • Wellness visits
  • Medication reviews
  • Chronic condition care
  • Screenings & vaccines

Your first visit.

New patients are welcome. Here is how it works.

  1. Call to set up a time

    Reach the front desk at (318) 600-4364 during office hours. We see patients by appointment, so please call ahead.

  2. Bring the basics

    A photo ID, your insurance card and a list of the medications you take. For children, bring their shot record.

  3. Come a few minutes early

    There is a little paperwork on a first visit. Ten minutes is plenty.

Insurance Coverage depends on your plan. Call and we will check yours first.
Payment Credit cards, debit cards and mobile pay are accepted.
Accessibility Wheelchair-accessible entrance, parking and restroom.
